How connecting works, and why it is safe

  • You sign in yourself. Every connection uses the tool's own secure sign-in page. You type your password into their page, never to us. We never see, receive, or store your passwords.
  • One tap at a time. The checklist shows exactly what is being connected and why, and nothing connects without your tap.
  • You can turn a connection off anytime. Every tool lets you disconnect in its own settings, instantly, no phone call and no questions.
  • Least access by default. We request only the access each tool needs to do its job, and you see it before you approve it.
