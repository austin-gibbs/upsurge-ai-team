## 2. What Happens Next

Your install takes place in five stages. Most clients are fully live within a few business days, and your only job throughout is a few small approvals. We handle everything else.

**Stage 1: We set up your command center.**
We build your private Surge Command app: your request board, your team page, and your activity view, branded for your business.
*What we need from you:* nothing yet. We will send you a link when it is ready to look at.

**Stage 2: We connect your tools.**
You link the apps your business already runs on: email, calendar, your CRM, and anything else your team should be able to work in. It takes a few minutes, one tool at a time, and you sign in yourself. More on this in the next section.
*What we need from you:* about fifteen minutes, and the sign ins you already use every day.

**Stage 3: We seat your team.**
We assign your specialists and configure them around your business: your services, your voice, your customers, your priorities.
*What we need from you:* nothing. If we spot a choice worth your opinion, we will bring it to you with a recommendation.

**Stage 4: We learn your business.**
We study your website, your materials, and your existing systems, then sit down with you for one short conversation to fill in the gaps: how you like things done, what great looks like, what to never do.
*What we need from you:* one 30 minute call, at whatever time suits you.

**Stage 5: You go live.**
Your team page lights up, your board is open, and we run your first real request end to end, usually the same day. From that moment, you are simply live.
*What we need from you:* your first request. Anything. Start with the task you have been putting off.

## 3. Connecting Your Tools

Connecting your tools is how your team gets its hands. It is simple, it is secure, and you stay in control the whole time.

You will see a checklist in your Surge Command app, one tool at a time:

- **Email** so your team can draft, send, and follow up with your approval rules in place
- **Calendar** so your team can book, prepare, and protect your schedule
- **Files and documents** so your team works from your real materials, not guesses
- **Your CRM or customer system** so follow up, pipelines, and records stay current
- **Your website and marketing tools** so pages, posts, and campaigns can ship without waiting on anyone
- **Anything else you rely on**: just tell us, and we will add it to the list

**How it works, and why it is safe:**

- **You sign in yourself.** Every connection uses the tool's own secure sign in page. You type your password into their page, never to us. We never see, receive, or store your passwords.
- **One tap at a time.** The checklist shows you exactly what is being connected and why, and nothing connects without your tap.
- **You can revoke access anytime.** Every tool lets you disconnect in its own settings, instantly, no phone call and no questions. The access simply stops.
- **Least access by default.** We request only the access each tool needs to do its job, and we will always tell you what that is before you approve it.

If anything on the list does not apply to your business, skip it. Your team works with whatever you connect, and you can add more later in seconds.

## 4. How to Work With Your Team

### Sending a request

There are three ways to put your team to work. Use whichever fits your day. They all land in the same place.

**Option A: Ask in your Surge Command app.**
Open your app, tap New Request, and write what you need in plain language. This is the fastest route for most clients, because your team sees it instantly and your board tracks it from there.

**Option B: Use your own project tool.**
If your business already runs on a project management system, keep using it. Your team watches the board you assign us to and picks work up from there, so nothing about your routine has to change.

**Option C: Just tell your team lead.**
Message your team lead the way you would message a chief of staff. A sentence is enough. We take it from there and log it properly on your behalf.

### What a good request looks like

You do not need to write a brief. That is our job. A good request is just a clear outcome and any fact we cannot guess.

> "Follow up with every lead from last week's open house who has not booked a tour yet. Friendly, short, and offer Thursday or Saturday times."

> "Build a landing page for the spring listing package. Use the photos in the Spring folder, match the website, and have it ready to review by Friday."

Notice what is not in there: no formatting, no technical detail, no step by step. Give us the destination and the one or two facts that matter. We work out the route.

### What happens after you submit

1. **You get an acknowledgment.** Quickly, every time, so you know it landed with a human standard of care.
2. **We turn it into a plan.** Your team lead converts your request into a precise work brief and assigns it to the right specialist. You will see the request appear on your board with a clear status.
3. **You can watch it move, or not.** Your Surge Command app shows every request as it progresses. Most clients glance at the team page over coffee and otherwise forget about it.
4. **It comes back finished.** Delivered work arrives ready to use, with a short note on what was done and anything we need your eye on. If something needs your decision along the way, we bring you the exact question and a recommendation, once, in one place.

### Turnaround

Most standard requests are completed the same business day they arrive, and larger builds are broken into pieces you can see and use as they land. If a request ever needs more time, parts from an outside party, or a decision from you, we will tell you early and plainly, never at the deadline. Speed matters to us, and so does never promising you a time we cannot keep.

## 5. Going Live, and Your First Week

When your install is complete, you will receive a short message from your team lead:

> **You are live.** Your team is seated, your tools are connected, and your Surge Command app is open. Send your first request whenever you are ready. We are.

Your first week settles into a rhythm most clients tell us they wish everything in their business followed:

- **A daily digest.** One short message each morning: what was finished yesterday, what is in motion today, and anything that needs your decision. Reading it takes about a minute.
- **A weekly review.** Once a week, a brief summary of the week: work delivered, time saved, what is coming next, and one or two suggestions from your team on where we could take more off your plate.
- **Your team page, anytime.** Open your app whenever you like to see your whole organization at a glance: who is on your team, what everyone is working on, and the steady pulse of work moving through.

By the end of week one, the goal is simple: asking your team should feel as natural as asking your best employee, and considerably faster.

## 6. Escalation and Support

Your team lead is your first call for everything: a question, a change, an urgent request, or a "drop everything" moment. Message your team lead inside your Surge Command app or through the contact channel we set up together at install, and a person on our side is accountable for the answer.

Behind your team lead stands UpSurge itself. If anything ever needs a human decision at our level, a contract question, a concern, a big new idea, your team lead brings in the UpSurge leadership team directly. You will never be handed a ticket number or asked to explain yourself twice.

For anything time sensitive, mark your request urgent in the app or say the word in your message. Urgent requests jump the queue and get an immediate acknowledgment, day or night.

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions

**Is my business information private?**
Yes. Your information lives inside your own install, walled off from every other client we serve. Your documents, customer details, and business facts are used to do your work and for nothing else. They are never shared with, shown to, or used for another client.

**Who can see my information?**
Only your assigned team and the UpSurge staff accountable for your account. Your team members each see what they need for their part of the work. No one else, and never the public.

**What exactly does my team learn about my business?**
Only what helps us serve you: your services, your customers, your standards, your preferences. The expertise your team brings, how to build, write, automate, market, and follow up at a professional level, comes from our side. Your private details never become part of anyone else's service.

**Can my staff use the team too?**
Absolutely. You can add team members from your business at any time, and you control what each person can request and see. Many clients start with the owner and add a manager or an assistant in the first week.

**What if we already use our own project management tool?**
Keep it. Your team can pick up work from the system you already run, and your Surge Command app still gives you the full picture in one place. There is no migration and no retraining for your staff.

**Can I change how my team works over time?**
Anytime. New tools, new priorities, new team members, new rules about what needs your approval. Tell your team lead and consider it done. Your setup is meant to grow with your business, not box it in.

**What happens if I want to pause or leave?**
You can disconnect any tool instantly from its own settings, and pausing or ending service is a conversation with our team, never a maze. Your data is yours, and we will help you take it with you.
