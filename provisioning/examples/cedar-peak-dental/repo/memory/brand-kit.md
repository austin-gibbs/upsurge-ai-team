## Approved claims list

This list starts empty. That is deliberate. No number, savings figure, testimonial, or outcome statement may appear in any client facing deliverable for Cedar Peak Dental unless it is on this list with its approval source.

How a claim gets on the list:

1. The claim is written exactly as it may be used, word for word.
2. The client approves it in writing, or it is taken character for character from the client's own published property, which is named as the source.
3. The entry is added below with the approval date and the approver or source.
4. A claim the client withdraws is marked withdrawn with the date. It is not deleted, so the team can see it was once approved and no longer is.
