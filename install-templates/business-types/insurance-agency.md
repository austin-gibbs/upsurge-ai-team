## Words this business uses

| General idea | Words to use | What it means here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Project | Client file | All the requests, policies, dates, messages, and next steps tied to one client or household. |
| Customer | Client | A person, family, or business the agency serves. A client may also be called a policyholder after coverage starts. |
| Main work item | Policy need | The coverage need being quoted, started, changed, renewed, or reviewed. |
| New person | Prospect | Someone who asked about coverage but is not yet a client. |
| Price request | Quote request | A request to gather facts and ask an approved company or system for pricing. A quote request is not a price promise. |
| Yearly check point | Renewal | The date when a policy needs review and a new term may begin. |
| Help after a loss | Claim help | The first steps, papers, and follow up after something is damaged, lost, or someone is hurt. |
| Done | Coverage in place | Coverage has started, the client has the papers and payment details, and the next renewal or review date is saved. |

- "Draft a kind check in for prospects who asked for a quote and have not replied."
- "Sort these service requests and tell me what fact is missing from each one."
- "Prepare the questions I should ask before this quote request goes out."
- "Write calm first steps for a client who needs claim help after a car accident."
- "Find proof of coverage requests that were promised but not marked sent."
- "Draft a welcome message for a new client that explains how to reach our office."

## Connect first

1. **Agency customer record system or CRM.** Client details, policy types, renewal dates, service requests, and follow ups need one home. After sign in, check client lists, policy fields, renewal dates, tags, and current work steps.
2. **Email.** Quote requests, policy papers, proof requests, and client replies often arrive here. After sign in, check shared inboxes, saved replies, and who handles each kind of message.
3. **Phone.** Clients often need a person quickly after a loss or a billing problem. After sign in, check the office number, call notes, text use, and after hours directions.
4. **Calendar.** Reviews, renewal calls, and client meetings need clear times. After sign in, check meeting types, reminders, office hours, and time zone.

## Questions we ask during setup

- Which kinds of coverage do you sell, and which do you not sell?
- Which states are you licensed to serve?
- Which companies or quoting tools may the team prepare work for?
- Who must approve every quote, coverage change, and client promise?
- What are the first steps when a client needs claim help?
- How early should renewal work begin, and how should proof of coverage requests be handled?

## Extra guardrails

- Automated help may gather facts and prepare a quote request. It must not state, guess, compare, or promise a rate, premium, deductible, discount, or coverage result.
- A licensed team member must approve quotes, coverage advice, policy changes, and claim guidance that goes beyond the agency's approved first steps.
- Do not tell a client that a loss is covered or not covered. Use the agency's approved claim directions and route the question to the right licensed person or company.
- Keep client details inside the tools the agency approves.
- If a request is urgent or someone may be unsafe, follow the agency's emergency and claim directions first.

## Client choices

You stay in control of the words and the flow. You can:

- call a client file, client, policy need, or renewal by a different name
- rename, add, remove, or reorder any board stage
- add or remove any work group
- save your own favorite starter requests
- choose a different tool to connect first
- tell the team to stop using any word that does not sound like your agency
