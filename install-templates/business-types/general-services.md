## Starter requests

- "Sort these new requests and tell me what detail is missing from each one."
- "Turn these notes into clear tasks with an owner and a next step."
- "Draft a friendly update for every client waiting on us."
- "Make a list of work that is late, waiting, or missing approval."
- "Write a weekly summary of finished work, open work, and what needs my decision."
- "Prepare a follow up for clients who have not replied."
- "Draft a thank you and review request for a happy client."

## Connect first

1. **Work tracker or CRM.** Requests, clients, jobs, owners, and next steps need one home. After sign in, check current lists, fields, owners, tags, and work steps.
2. **Email.** Client requests, files, approvals, and follow ups often live here. After sign in, check shared inboxes, saved replies, and who answers each kind of message.
3. **Calendar.** Calls, meetings, deadlines, and delivery dates need one time view. After sign in, check calendars, meeting types, reminders, and time zone.
4. **Phone.** Quick questions and urgent requests often start with a call or text. After sign in, check the business number, call notes, text use, and after hours directions.
5. **File storage.** Current documents, photos, and finished work should be easy to find. After sign in, check folder names, sharing rules, and where final work belongs.
6. **Accounting.** Quotes, invoices, deposits, and paid or unpaid status should be clear without exposing bank details. After sign in, check record types, payment terms, and who handles money questions.

## Questions we ask during setup

- What services do you sell, and who are they for?
- What words do you use for a project, a customer, and one piece of work?
- What are the normal steps from a new request to finished work?
- Which requests need your approval before the team replies or delivers?
- Which tools hold the truth for clients, time, files, and payments?
- What work do you most want to hand off first?

## What we look for first

| Source | What we look for | Where it helps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Website | Services, who the business serves, team names, locations, hours, common questions, and review themes | Setup words, public answers, and first follow ups |
| Work tracker or CRM | Current lists, fields, owners, tags, work steps, and open requests | Board setup and daily work sorting |
| Email | Common requests, approvals, saved replies, and delivery notes | Message starters and missing detail checks |
| Calendar | Meeting types, work blocks, deadlines, and reminders | Scheduling and due date checks |
| File storage and accounting | Folder order, final file locations, quote and invoice states, and payment terms | Clean delivery and clear money follow ups |

## Extra guardrails

- Standard client approval and privacy rules apply.
- Do not promise a price, date, result, or change in scope until the client approves it.
- Keep private client details inside the tools the client approves.
- If the work touches health, money, legal rights, safety, or regulated services, use the client's approved expert and rules before acting.

## Client choices

You stay in control of the words and the flow. You can:

- call a project, client, job, or service by a different name
- rename, add, remove, or reorder any board stage
- add or remove any work group
- save your own favorite starter requests
- choose a different tool to connect first
- tell the team to stop using any word that does not sound like your business
