## Starter requests

- "Follow up with every new inquiry from this week and list anyone who still needs an answer."
- "Make a list of applications that are missing papers and say what each one needs."
- "Draft a friendly reminder for students or families with a tour or meeting coming up."
- "Write a welcome message for new students that lists their first steps before the start date."
- "Check the next term list and tell me which enrolled students have not confirmed."
- "Turn these meeting notes into tasks with an owner and a due date."
- "Make a weekly report that shows inquiries, tours, applications, enrollments, and starts."
- "Draft a check in for students who started this week and may need help."

## Connect first

1. **Student record system or CRM.** Inquiries, applications, papers, enrollment status, and start dates need one main record. After sign in, choose which system is the trusted student record, then check program fields, current steps, owners, and start dates.
2. **Email.** Questions, paper requests, meeting notes, and welcome messages often live here. After sign in, check shared inboxes, saved replies, and who answers each kind of message.
3. **Phone.** Students and families often need quick help with times, papers, and next steps. After sign in, check the main number, call notes, text use, and after hours directions.
4. **Calendar.** Tours, meetings, interviews, and start dates need clear times. After sign in, check meeting types, staff calendars, reminders, holidays, and time zone.

## Questions we ask during setup

- Which programs do you offer, and who is each one for?
- What are the next term start dates and the last day to enroll?
- What are the exact steps from first interest to enrolled?
- Which papers are required, and who checks them?
- Who may approve a place in a program, a payment plan, or a start date change?
- Which tours, meetings, or interviews do you offer, and how long do they take?
- What should a student or family receive right after enrolling?

## Extra guardrails

- Keep student and family details inside the systems the client approves.
- Share student details only with staff who need them for the task.
- Do not promise a place in a program, a start date, a payment plan, or a result until the client's approved staff member confirms it.
- Do not change an official student record unless the client has given a clear rule and the change can be checked.
- Use the client's approved words for program outcomes. Never promise a job, grade, license, or income result.

## Client choices

You stay in control of the words and the flow. You can:

- call an enrollment, student, application, program, or term by a different name
- rename, add, remove, or reorder any board stage
- add or remove any work group
- save your own favorite starter requests
- choose a different tool to connect first
- tell the team to stop using any word that does not sound like your school or program
