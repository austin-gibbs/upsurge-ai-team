## Words this business uses

| General idea | Words to use | What it means here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Project | Transaction | All the steps, people, papers, dates, and messages tied to one purchase or sale. |
| Customer | Client | A buyer, seller, renter, or landlord the team is actively helping. |
| Main work item | Listing or home search | A listing is a property for sale or rent. A home search is the work of finding the right property for a buyer. |
| New person | Lead | Someone who asked about a property, a home value, buying, selling, or renting but is not yet a client. |
| Property helper word | Showing | A visit to view a property. |
| Finished result | Closing | The sale is complete. |
| Done | Closed | The sale has closed, final papers are complete, keys have changed hands, and the client has received a closing follow up. |

## Starter requests

- "Follow up with every new lead from yesterday and list anyone who still needs a reply."
- "Write a friendly check in for each active listing that has had no showing this week."
- "Make a list of buyers who need new homes that match what they want."
- "Put together today's showing plan with addresses, times, and client names."
- "Check every file under contract and tell me which date or paper is missing."
- "Draft an update for each seller about showings, feedback, and next steps."
- "Write a closing day message for the client and a reminder for our team."
- "Make a list of past clients I should call this week for reviews or referrals."

## Connect first

1. **Customer record system or CRM.** This holds leads, clients, listings, follow ups, and past clients in one place. After sign in, check the main lists, tags, owners, and current board steps.
2. **Phone.** Calls and texts are often the fastest way to reach leads and clients. After sign in, check the business number, call notes, text history, and who answers after hours.
3. **Email.** Listing updates, contracts, lender notes, and closing messages often live here. After sign in, check the main inboxes, saved replies, and signature details.
4. **Calendar.** Showings, listing appointments, inspections, and closings need one shared view of time. After sign in, check appointment types, team calendars, reminders, and time zone.
5. **Listing feed or IDX.** Current property details should come from the trusted property source, not from old copies. After sign in, check which listings are included, how often details refresh, and which fields the team may use.

## Questions we ask during setup

- Which areas, towns, and property types do you serve most?
- Where do most new leads come from?
- What should happen in the first hour after a new lead arrives?
- What are your steps from a signed listing to a live listing?
- Who handles contracts, lender updates, inspections, and closing dates?
- Which words, promises, or property claims must the team never use without your approval?

## Extra guardrails

- Never invent a property fact, price, date, showing result, or buyer interest.
- Property facts must come from the trusted listing source or the client.
- Only the client or their licensed broker may approve contract terms and legal wording.
- Marketing must describe the property and the facts fairly. Do not write messages that push people away because of who they are.
- Do not promise a sale price, a closing date, or buyer demand unless the client has approved those words.

## Client choices

You stay in control of the words and the flow. You can:

- call a transaction, client, listing, or closing by a different name
- rename, add, remove, or reorder any board stage
- add or remove any work group
- save your own favorite starter requests
- choose a different tool to connect first
- tell the team to stop using any word that does not sound like your business
